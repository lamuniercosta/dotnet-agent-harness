1	# team-restart
2	
3	Run this when a task is finished and the team is about to start fresh. It
4	clears every seat's context. The task's notes are already gone — step 9.5 of
5	`task-pipeline` deletes them. It does **not**
6	re-recruit and does **not** rewrite roles — terminals, role assignments and
7	the connections are durable and already correct.
8	
9	You must be the Maestro to run this. Confirm with `maestri list`.
10	
11	**In a chain** (see `task-chain`) you run this note yourself between tasks,
12	sections 1 to 4 only. Section 5 waits until the chain ends: you are the
13	chain's continuity, and clearing yourself mid-chain is how a chain gets lost.
14	
15	## 1. Confirm the canvas is clear
16	
17	The task's notes should already be gone. Step 9.5 of `task-pipeline` publishes
18	the plan to the ticket and then deletes every note for the task, before you
19	get here. Your job is to check that it happened, not to redo it:
20	
21	    maestri list
22	
23	If the fichário "Task notes" holds anything besides `task-notes-keeper`, or
24	any `task-DEV-###`, `plan-DEV-###`, `findings-*`, `recon-*`,
25	`adjudication-*`, `merge-bar-*`, `pr-body-*` or `trust-*` note is loose on
26	the canvas, step 9.5 did not complete. **Stop and finish it** — publish the plan to the ticket,
27	verify the comment matches the note byte for byte, then delete. Do not archive
28	them, and do not restart the team around them. A note that survives a restart
29	is a note that resurfaces in the next task, and the next task is not entitled
30	to the last one's context.
31	
32	Leave `harness-team-charter`, `task-pipeline`, `team-restart`, `task-chain`,
33	the fichário "Active notes", `task-notes-keeper` and the fichário "Task
34	notes" itself alone. They are standing, not task notes; the keeper is what
35	keeps the seats' wiring to "Task notes" alive between tasks. In a chain,
36	`chain` is standing too, until the chain's last reap.
37	
38	## 2. Clear each seat's context
39	
40	There is no `maestri clear`. Clearing means sending the host's own clear
41	command to the terminal as keystrokes, and the hosts do not agree on what
42	that is.
43	
44	| Seat | Host | What works | What does not (observed 2026-09-11) |
45	| --- | --- | --- | --- |
46	| Keel | `codex` | `recruit --replace` with the full launch line | `/clear` and `/new` sent as keystrokes were not consumed |
47	| Wisp | `gemini` | `recruit --replace` | `/clear` unverified — do not rely on it |
48	| Anvil, Gauge | cursor `agent` | `recruit --replace` | `/clear` not observed clearing |
49	| Quill, Compass | `agy` | `recruit --replace` | no `/clear` command exists |
50	| Cog, Ledger | `junie` | `recruit --replace` | `/new` is read as a task prompt ("The user provided /new but no DEV-### task id") |
51	| Sentry, Rigger | `opencode` | `recruit --replace` | `/new` is treated as prompt text |

53	No host's clear keystroke is verified. Clear every seat with
54	`maestri recruit "<Seat>" --replace "<Seat>" --command "<full launch line from the table below>"`;
55	the keystroke path is kept only for the day a host is confirmed to honour it.
56	
57	For a host whose clear command is confirmed:
58	
59	    maestri ask "Keel" --raw "/clear
60	"
61	
62	Confirm by looking at the terminal: a fresh prompt with no prior turns visible.
63	If the clear did not take, use the fallback.
64	
65	**Use `maestri check "Seat"` to confirm a clear.** It reads the seat's current
66	terminal output without sending input. Confirm a fresh prompt with no prior
67	turns visible, and do not treat unsent text in the TUI input area as a completed
68	clear.
69	
70	**Universal fallback.** Works on any host. It kills the agent process and
71	relaunches it in the same terminal, so the terminal node, its role assignment
72	and every connection survive untouched:
73	
74	    maestri ask "Anvil" --raw "\x03"
75	    maestri ask "Anvil" --raw "\x03"
76	    maestri ask "Anvil" --raw "<launch command>
77	"

97	Things that table will not tell you, and that have each cost a run:
98	
99	- **Cog carries no `--effort` flag on purpose.** Junie parses it and ignores
100	  it -- see below. A flag in the launch line that does nothing reads like the
101	  effort is set when it is not.
102	- **Both OpenCode seats now run the same model.** `opencode.jsonc` holds a
103	  single global `model` shared by every OpenCode seat; Sentry and Rigger are
104	  both on `openrouter/z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`, so nothing differs today. The day
105	  one of them drops to its replacement, that seat **must** carry `--model` or
106	  it silently comes up on the other's model. `-m` is the short form and is a
107	  global flag on the TUI command, not just on `run`.
108	- **`agy` has no `--auto` and no `--trust`.** There is no flag that grants
109	  Quill or Compass permission; grants live in
110	  `~/.gemini/antigravity-cli/settings.json` under `permissions.allow`, as
111	  directory-form `read_file(<abs dir>)` / `write_file(<abs dir>)` pairs.
112	  Containment is real, not string-prefix: the repo grant does **not** cover
113	  the sibling `F:\Dev\dotnet-agent-harness.worktrees`, which needs its own
114	  pair and is why Quill used to halt mid-task on a permission prompt. Both
115	  pairs are in place as of 2026-09-08. Compass is read-only and needs only the
116	  read pair, which it already has. If an agy seat still prompts, `--mode
117	  accept-edits` exists on the launch line, but fix the grant first.
118	- **Ledger's effort is in Junie's settings, not on the launch line.** Junie
119	  ignores `--effort`; `~/.junie/settings.json` -> `effortPerModel` maps
120	  `gpt-5.6-luna` to `high`. Verify `high` in the status bar after every
121	  relaunch. Ledger is on Junie to keep the Codex pool for Keel; if Junie's
122	  credits run dry, its chain falls to `codex -m gpt-5.6-luna` and Keel's
123	  weekly bar has to carry both.
124	- **Wisp's `--yolo` is not a licence.** The Scout is read-only by role, and
125	  the role prompt is what enforces it; the flag exists so the seat does not
126	  stall on an approval prompt for a `maestri` command. Gemini's
127	  `--approval-mode plan` blocks shell too, which is why it is not used.
128	
129	**Never change a seat's model by keystroke.** Maestri keeps each terminal's
130	original command in `workspace.json`; interrupting the agent and typing a new
131	launch line changes what is running but not what is stored, so the seat
132	silently reverts the next time Maestri starts that terminal. Use:
133	
134	    maestri recruit "Seat" --command "<new launch command>" --replace "Seat"
135	
136	It restarts the process in the same node and keeps the role assignment,
137	connections, canvas position and routines. Chat history does not carry over --
138	which is exactly what you want here. Verify afterwards by reading the model off
139	the seat's status bar, and confirm the stored command changed in
140	`workspace.json`.
141	
142	**`--replace` without `--command` reuses the stored command, and the stored
143	command is whatever the last recruit passed** -- not this table. Always pass
144	`--command` with the full launch line from the table above. After every
145	recruit, `maestri check "<Seat>"` must show the host's own prompt and the
146	status-bar model must match the table before the seat receives any ask; if it
147	does not, recruit again. This table is the source of truth for seating: when
148	the user re-seats a host by hand, the row here changes in the same breath
149	(on 2026-09-11 a stale Ledger row put Ledger back on Codex at the first
150	restart).
151	
152	The Ctrl-C fallback below is for **clearing context only**, never for changing
153	a model.
154	
155	**A cursor `--model` leaks to every unpinned cursor seat.** `cursor-agent`
156	persists the flag to `~/.cursor/cli-config.json`, so it is a global default and
157	not a per-invocation choice. Both cursor seats above (Anvil, Gauge) are pinned
158	explicitly, which is what protects them; launching a bare `agent` anywhere on
159	this machine re-points the file, and any seat that is not pinned picks that up
160	on its next start. The Conductor's own Cursor floor (`composer-2.5`) is pinned
161	for the same reason.
162	
163	**Junie effort is not settable from the launch line.** `--effort high` and
164	`--effort=high` are both parsed and both ignored. Effort lives in
165	`~/.junie/settings.json` under `effortPerModel`, a JSON string mapping model
166	alias -> low|medium|high. Built-in aliases are keyed bare (`gemini-3.1-flash-lite`);
167	custom profiles are keyed `custom:<filename>`.
168	
169	**A model absent from that map runs at LOW, silently.** Nothing in the UI says
170	so; the only trace is `Effort=low` in `~/.junie/logs/`. That is why Sentry
171	dropped when it moved off GLM 5.2 -- the new model simply had no entry. It is
172	also why DEV-116 got a review from Ledger that cited line ranges which do not
173	exist: `gpt-5.6-luna` was unmapped. Both it and `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` were
174	added to the map on 2026-09-05, so Cog's head is mapped; Sentry's replacement
175	`grok-4.3` must be checked before it is ever used.
176	
177	The map is read at process start, so editing it changes nothing until the seat
178	is restarted. Check the status bar after a relaunch.
179	
180	**OpenCode has the same trap in a different place.** Reasoning effort sits at
181	`provider.openrouter.options.extraBody.reasoning` in
182	`~/.config/opencode/opencode.jsonc`. Model-level `extraBody` is silently
183	dropped, so that setting is global across every OpenCode seat at once.
184	
185	**When you learn something this note gets wrong, edit it.** The clear commands
186	above are the intended host commands; Sentry's `/new` was not accepted in
187	this run and needed the universal fallback. The `agy` and `codex` rows are
188	unverified as of 2026-09-11 — the first restart that confirms them should
189	change their status here. Clear verification uses `maestri check "Seat"`; it
190	reads the current terminal without sending input.
191	
192	Require a fresh prompt with no prior turns visible, and do not treat unsent TUI
193	input as a completed clear.
194	
195	That is part of the job, not an optional extra. This note should get faster
196	every time it runs.

198	## 3. Re-anchor every seat
199	
200	A cleared agent may or may not still hold its role prompt — that depends on how
201	Maestri injects it, and it is not safe to assume. What it certainly does **not**
202	hold is its codename. A model knows its host; nothing in its context tells it
203	the external label the canvas hung on it. On DEV-116, 5 of 8 seats got the
204	codename wrong on the first probe -- Quill said "Gemini CLI", Wisp said
205	"opencode", Ledger and Compass said "Junie", Sentry invented "Bramble", Rigger
206	said nothing -- while every one of them stated its *role* correctly. The role
207	prompt had survived the clear. Only the name had not.
208	
209	**So assign the codename. Do not probe for it.** Probing harder has never fixed
210	this and cannot: there is nothing for the seat to remember. Telling all ten who
211	they are costs one batch and one turn, and everything downstream depends on it
212	-- the charter requires every ask to open `[from <YourCodename>]`, and a seat
213	that does not know its own name either omits the tag or invents one, which is
214	indistinguishable from an unattributed instruction.

239	### 3b. Grade the reply on the lane, not the name
240	
241	You just supplied the name, so a seat echoing it proves nothing. What the reply
242	has to show is that the *lane* survived the clear, and that the seat is on its
243	chain. Three answers are a stop:
244	
245	- A seat that states a **different lane's** duties -- Gauge saying it writes
246	  code, a reviewer saying it adjudicates. Re-point it at its role record before
247	  it gets work.
248	- A seat that is **silent or reports its host out of usage**. Rigger was silent
249	  here and Anvil and Cog both reported Cursor "out of usage" -- that is a dry
250	  wallet, and under the charter it is a halt, not a re-probe. In a chain, a
251	  halt here ends the chain (see `task-chain` §4).
252	- A seat whose reported model appears **nowhere in its chain**. That is a seat
253	  that reverted to a stored command or a leaked default; fix the command with
254	  `recruit --replace`, never by keystroke.

256	## 4. Do not
257	
258	- Do not re-recruit. A full rebuild costs ten terminals and eleven connections
259	  and risks losing wiring, to gain nothing a clear does not already give you.
260	- Do not rewrite role records. They are current.
261	- Do not archive notes, and do not recreate an `Archive` stack. Task notes are
262	  deleted in step 9.5 of `task-pipeline`; the ticket holds the plan and the PR
263	  holds the findings.
264	- Do not touch `harness-team-charter`.
265	
266	## 5. Clear yourself last
267	
268	Everything above happens before you clear your own context. Then report to the
269	user: which seats cleared cleanly, which needed the fallback, and any that
270	failed re-anchoring. The user clears you after reading that report.

272	**Not in a chain.** Between chained tasks, stop after section 4, write the
